# Managing Products ## Adding products 1. Go to **Products** page → click **Add Product** 2. Fill in: name, price, short description, emoji/icon, badge label (hot/new/sale) 3. Save ## Why add products? - The bot uses your product catalog to answer price and availability questions — in both keyword rules and AI mode - When a customer asks about a specific product by name, the bot pulls info automatically ## Tips - Add your 3–5 best-selling products first so the bot can recommend them right away - In auto-reply rules, use these variables to reference your products dynamically: - `{product_name}` → replaced with first product name - `{product_price}` → replaced with first product price - `{product_list}` → shows up to 5 products (emoji + name + price)
