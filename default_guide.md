# Customer Management — CRM / Kanban / Lead Scoring

## Managing customers
- **Customers** page: view list, search, filter, export CSV.
- Click **Add Customer** to add manually (name, phone, source, product interest).
- Bulk status change or bulk delete supported.

## Kanban Board (drag & drop)
- **Kanban Board** page splits customers into 4 columns: **New → Hot → Close → Cold**.
- Drag a customer card between columns to update their status. Every move is recorded in their timeline.

## Lead Scoring (automatic potential rating)
- The system automatically scores customers based on their message content (price inquiry, purchase intent, phone number provided...).
- **Lead Scoring** page ranks customers highest to lowest — prioritize your "hottest" leads.
- Customers who reach the threshold are automatically promoted to Hot / Close status.

## Customer Timeline
- Click on any customer to see their full timeline: messages, score events, staff actions — complete care history at a glance.

## Tips
- Keyboard shortcut **Ctrl+K** to jump quickly to the customer search bar.
