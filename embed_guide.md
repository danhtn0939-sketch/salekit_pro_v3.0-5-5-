# Getting Started with SalesKit Pro

SalesKit Pro automates your sales: multi-channel chatbot + CRM + shared inbox + analytics.

## Where to start (4 recommended steps)
1. **Add products** (Products page) — so the bot has something to recommend.
2. **Create auto-reply rules** (Auto Reply Rules) — price, shipping, warranty answers.
3. **Enable AI** (AI Agent Config) — lets the bot handle unexpected questions naturally (Gemini free tier available).
4. **Connect a channel** (Channel Settings) or **embed the widget** on your website.

## Main sections
- **Dashboard**: summary stats and overview.
- **Customers / Kanban / Lead Scoring**: manage and nurture your leads.
- **Shared Inbox**: reply to messages from all channels in one place.
- **Bot Preview / Auto Reply Rules / AI Agent Config**: configure chatbot behavior.
- **Embed Widget / Channel Settings**: connect bot to website and social media.
- **Billing & Plans**: upgrade your subscription.
