# Billing & Plan Upgrade

## Available plans
Free / Basic / Pro / Enterprise — differ in customer limit, products, rules, and features.

## How to upgrade (domestic — bank transfer)
1. Go to **Billing & Plans**.
2. Choose a plan → click **Pay via Bank Transfer (VND)**.
3. Scan the QR code with your banking app and transfer the exact amount — **keep the payment reference in the description exactly as shown**.
4. The system confirms automatically once the transfer is received; your plan activates immediately.

## International customers
- **Pay with Stripe (USD)**: pay with Visa/Mastercard — plan activates automatically (if Stripe is enabled by the platform).
- **International Transfer**: PayPal / Wise / card — copy the transfer details, include the order reference in the description. Plan is activated after manual confirmation.

## Notes
- After transferring, your order shows status "Pending" until confirmed.
- If you've paid but the plan hasn't activated, contact support with your order reference.
